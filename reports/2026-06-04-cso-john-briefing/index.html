Legal Alignment

Communication Strategy with Legal

Objective: shift the conversation from “approval of individual activities” to “co-design of a controllable CSO governance model”.
Decision needed: approach, content, timing

1. Current legal requirements are constraining business readiness

1

2
Market strategy and activities are insufficient; NVS events reduced by ~90%
3
Channel support remains ambiguous

2. Four-step legal communication strategy

Probe root cause

Clarify whether the concern is lack of trust in KP, perceived conflict with Channel, or structural ambiguity in CSO governance.

Target: real objection identified
Confirm legal purpose

Understand whether Legal aims to stop the model, narrow the scope, or find a compliant operating solution.

Target: common problem statement
Align on New Governance Framework

Introduce the new six-dimension governance framework as a risk-control system, not as a way to bypass compliance.

Target: governance framework accepted
Go deeper on specific practices

Use concrete topics — training assessment, marketing support, DM training, speaker invitation — to define red lines and workable SOPs.

Target: actionable guidance

3. Discussion: John / Ethan / Legal communication design

Legal support required

  • Roadmap: Overall guidance on the CSO governance roadmap and red-line principles.
  • Contract: Professional recommendation on service-item definition for future contract design.
  • Marketing: Clear guidance on marketing strategy communication, speaker invitation, knowledge assessment and DM training.
  • Decision: Fast decision on reps. registration / onboarding tracking requirement.
  • SOP: Reviewable SOPs for meetings, documentation, issue escalation and audit trail.

Positioning to Legal: “We are not asking Legal to approve a high-risk CSO model. We are asking Legal to help build a lower-risk, transparent and auditable cooperation-governance system.”
